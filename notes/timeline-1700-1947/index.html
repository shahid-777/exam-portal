“A moment comes, which comes but rarely in history…”

At the stroke of the midnight hour, while the world sleeps, India awakes to life and freedom. Two hundred and forty-seven years after Aurangzeb's death set an empire on its long decline, a new republic rises from the twilight — carrying with it the scars, and the promise, of the centuries before.
